## Forest scene assets

- **Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit [Standard]** (all 68 FBX models and the 15 textures they use, `unity/Assets/App/Forest/Art/MegaKit`):
  CC0 1.0, license text kept in `unity/Assets/App/Forest/Art/MegaKit/License_Standard.txt`. It replaced the first forest
  (Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack, 45 models), which is archived in `archive/quaternius-ultimate-nature-pack/` outside `Assets`.

- **Ambient audio**: synthesised by `ForestAudioSynth` (original, no samples).
